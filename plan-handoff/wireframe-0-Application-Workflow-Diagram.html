3 · AI extraction
Reconciliation Orchestrator Loads session rules, identifies source types, and delegates extraction.
Parallel source-specific delegation
Invoice Extraction Agent Invoice number, vendor, date, amount, currency, status, and evidence.
Payment Extraction Agent Payment reference, payee, date, amount, currency, status, and evidence.
Invoice Processing Review extracted fields, validation flags, evidence, and session corrections.
4 · Validation
Normalize records Standardize dates, vendor names, references, currencies, and decimals.
Required fields Check identifiers, amount, currency, date, and transaction status.
Duplicate detection Compare reference, amount, currency, and near-date clusters.
Record eligible for matching?
No · Create exception Missing data, duplicate, invalid status, or unsupported currency.
5 · Reconciliation
Candidate scoring Reference, vendor, currency, amount variance, date variance, and status.
Apply session rules 0.50% amount tolerance, 100 cap, 3-day window, and confidence thresholds.
Exact match All required matching fields agree. Mark reconciled in session.
Tolerance match Variance is within policy and confidence is at least 95%.
Suggested match Confidence is 70–94%. Human review is mandatory.
Exception No valid candidate, policy breach, duplicate, or confidence below 70%.
6 · Investigation
Exception Management Triage reason, severity, source evidence, variance, and failed rules.
Suggested resolution AI proposes next steps with confidence and supporting rule evidence.
Analyst investigation Add notes, correct session data, accept guidance, or propose a resolution.
Sensitive or uncertain?
Route to Approval Centre Required for suggestions, duplicates, overrides, policy breaches, and high value.
7 · Human approval
Approver reviews evidence Compare source records, rule results, confidence, risk, and analyst notes.
Mandatory decision note Require rationale and confirm the action is simulation-only.

Approve or reject simulated match

Approve → session status becomes reconciled
Reject → retain or create exception
Return → analyst queue with feedback

Hard safety stop

No payment release
No balance change
